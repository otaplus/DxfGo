# Built-in help
help.title=Safe toolpath guide
help.text=SAFE CNC 2D TOOLPATH GUIDE
+
+1. Z safety planes
+   • Safe Z (Clearance): height at which all G0 rapid moves are performed.
+     It avoids clamps, screws and fixtures.
+   • Approach Z (Retract): intermediate height before plunging at a
+     controlled feed.
+
+2. Inside-First rule
+   • The program detects inner holes and pockets and machines them BEFORE
+     the outer perimeter. If the outside is cut first, the part comes loose
+     and may cause a collision.
+
+3. Multiple passes
+   • The total depth is divided into even layers that never exceed the
+     stepdown, protecting the cutter.
+
+4. Holding tabs
+   • They leave small material bridges on the last pass so the part does not
+     fly off when the cut finishes.
+
+5. Cutter radius compensation
+   • Outer profile: offsets the cutter outward (+R).
+   • Inner profile: offsets the cutter inward (-R).
+   • On the line: cuts directly along the vector (engraving, laser, plasma).
+
+6. Automatic checks before cutting
+   • No G0 move in XY below safe Z.
+   • Spindle on (M3) before touching material.
+   • Z plunge at a controlled feed.
+   • Minimum depth within the programmed value.
+   • Final retraction and shutdown (M5).
+
+7. 2D mode (no Z axis)
+   • Removes every Z word from the program: no passes, no retractions and
+     no holding tabs.
+   • Intended for oxy-fuel torches that do not lift the Z axis and only turn
+     the oxygen off during rapids.
+   • Drilling becomes a simple punch point.
+
+8. Modal codes
+   • They insert custom commands before and after each G00 and each G02/G03,
+     for example to open or close the oxygen.
+   • Snippets are inserted without being reinterpreted, so they may contain
+     several M commands or even movements.
